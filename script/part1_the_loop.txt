You open the app. You tell yourself a number. Five minutes. Ten, maybe.

Then the numbers stop meaning anything.

And here's the strange part. When you finally put the phone down, you try to remember what you watched.

Most of it, you can't.

A few seconds of a recipe you'll never make. Someone's dog. An argument you weren't part of. A clip you've probably already seen a version of, three scrolls ago.

So here's the question this video is actually about: If most of what you watched wasn't that interesting… why didn't you stop?
