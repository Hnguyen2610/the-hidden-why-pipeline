So back to the question from the start. Why didn't you stop at five minutes?

Not because you lack willpower.

Not because your brain is broken.

Because somewhere between infinite scroll, autoplay, a feed built around what already works on you, and a notification pulling you back the moment you leave — the app never actually gave you a moment to decide.

The honest version of the question isn't "why can't you stop scrolling."

It's: why is stopping so much harder than starting?
